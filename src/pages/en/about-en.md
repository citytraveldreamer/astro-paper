Hello, I am the *The Flâneur* (都市遊魂).

I am a freelance content creator. My work here primarily focuses on travel, intertwined with moments of lifestyle and food discoveries.

### The Story Behind the Name

I love traveling and wandering through different cities—walking, thinking, and daydreaming. 

In Cantonese, the name "遊魂" (wandering soul) sounds identical to "遊雲" (wandering clouds), which beautifully describes a state where your mind drifts away. This perfectly captures my most authentic state when exploring a city: no rushed itineraries, just following my heart and letting my thoughts wander alongside my footsteps in unfamiliar streets.

### Photography & Daily Life

Armed with my Sony camera, I love weaving through streets and alleys. I have a deep appreciation for street snapshots—capturing the raw, unscripted fragments of daily life. To me, the most ordinary moments often conceal the most authentic soul of a city.

### My Hope for You

I created this space not just to document my footprints, but with the hope that you might take away something meaningful and inspiring after reading my words and viewing my photos. Whether it's a spark of travel inspiration or a new perspective on everyday life.

Welcome to my personal space. If you resonate with this pace of life, feel free to connect with me on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/citytraveldreamer), and let's wander together.
